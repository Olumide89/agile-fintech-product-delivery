# Decision Record — Urgent Regulatory Requirement

**Decision ID:** DEC-001  
**Sprint:** 5  
**Decision owner:** Product Owner, Scrum Master and Compliance Lead  
**Simulation scenario**

## 1. Situation

During Sprint 5, the compliance team identified an urgent regulatory requirement relating to customer data access, consent and deletion. The requirement must be addressed before release.

## 2. Impact

| Area | Impact |
|---|---|
| Scope | Two new compliance stories added; one lower-priority reporting enhancement deferred |
| Schedule | Sprint 5 scope increased from 20 to 24 story points |
| Quality | Testing and security remediation protected |
| Stakeholders | Product roadmap communication required |
| Release | Release date retained, subject to successful compliance testing |

## 3. Options considered

| Option | Description | Advantages | Disadvantages | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Defer compliance work | Move the requirement to the next release | Protects current scope | Creates regulatory and reputational risk | Rejected |
| B. Compress testing | Deliver all planned features and reduce testing time | Preserves feature scope | High quality and compliance risk | Rejected |
| C. Reprioritize backlog | Deliver compliance work and defer a lower-value reporting enhancement | Protects compliance, quality and release date | Delays a customer-facing enhancement | **Selected** |
| D. Extend the release | Add a seventh sprint | Protects all scope | Delays benefits and increases cost | Rejected |

## 4. Decision

Adopt **Option C**. The team will deliver the regulatory requirement in Sprint 5, defer the lower-priority monthly trend enhancement to the next release, and retain the six-sprint release window.

## 5. Follow-up actions

- Update the product backlog and release plan.
- Communicate the deferred enhancement to stakeholders.
- Track compliance testing as a release gate.
- Review the decision in the Sprint 5 retrospective.
