# Product Vision and Roadmap

**Simulation scenario** | Fintech budgeting application | Six-sprint delivery

## Product vision

Help customers understand, plan and control their spending through a simple, secure budgeting application that turns transactions into clear, actionable insight.

## Target users

- Young professionals managing monthly spending
- Households tracking shared expenses
- Customers who want clearer visibility of subscriptions, bills and discretionary spending

## Value proposition

The application gives users a single place to categorize transactions, set budgets, monitor progress and receive practical alerts before overspending occurs.

## Product goals

1. Enable users to connect and categorize transactions.
2. Let users create monthly budgets by category.
3. Show clear budget-versus-actual performance.
4. Provide alerts when spending approaches or exceeds a budget.
5. Meet applicable data-protection and regulatory requirements.
6. Deliver a reliable, usable release after six sprints.

## Release roadmap

| Sprint | Theme | Key outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Sprint 1 | Foundation | User accounts, transaction import and basic categorization |
| Sprint 2 | Budgeting | Category budgets, monthly budget creation and dashboard |
| Sprint 3 | Insight | Budget-versus-actual reporting and spending trends |
| Sprint 4 | Alerts | Threshold alerts and notification preferences |
| Sprint 5 | Compliance and quality | Regulatory requirement, security testing and defect reduction |
| Sprint 6 | Release | Performance improvements, final acceptance testing and release readiness |

## Success measures

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Transaction categorization accuracy | At least 90% |
| Budget creation completion rate | At least 80% of new users |
| Alert delivery time | Within five minutes of threshold breach |
| Critical defects at release | Zero |
| Regulatory controls tested | 100% of applicable requirements |
